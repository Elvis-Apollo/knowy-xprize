## Knowy: AI account operations for small service businesses

Small agencies, consultancies, recruiting firms, and managed-service providers often manage client accounts through a patchwork of meeting notes, chat messages, documents, issue trackers, and individual memory. Preparing a reliable client update requires a skilled employee to reconstruct what happened, determine whether the account is healthy, identify the most important risk, and decide what the team should do next. That repeated coordination work limits how many customers a small team can serve.

Knowy is an AI account-operations agent that turns selected client evidence into a current, cited account brief and a concrete next action. The product retrieves only information relevant to the account question, checks whether an existing intelligence object is still fresh, and then uses Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI to classify the account as on track, at risk, blocked, or needing review. Gemini explains the decision, cites the supplied evidence, and recommends one operational next action. Knowy saves that decision with its source versions so it can be reused until the underlying evidence changes.

This is different from a generic chatbot. The AI is responsible for a recurring business decision: determining account health and the next priority action. The application controls the safety boundary. Code retrieves records, compares versions, decides whether stored intelligence is fresh, validates the structured model output, persists citations, and records every execution. Humans remain responsible for connecting customer information, reviewing recommendations, correcting evidence, approving external communication, and handling sensitive escalations.

Knowy runs with Gemini in production. Each agent execution records the provider, model, account, decision, evidence count, reuse or refresh mode, and timestamp. This gives our team an operational audit trail and gives customers a way to understand when a conclusion was created, reused, or refreshed. When a source record changes, Knowy identifies the stale dependency and regenerates the affected account brief instead of repeatedly rebuilding all company knowledge.
